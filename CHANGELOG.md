## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-21

### Added

- Bestway Wi-Fi spas (Lay-Z-Spa, SaluSpa) and Flowclear Smart Touch pool filter pumps through the Bestway cloud, with the account of the app they work with: Bestway Smart Hub (V1, Europe or United States) and Bestway Connect (V2, Europe, United States or China, e-mail and password: no sharing QR code needed); both accounts at once if needed.
- One device per spa, named as in the app: water temperature, target temperature (20 to 40 °C, spas set to Fahrenheit converted), heater, heating now, power, filter pump, bubbles (on / off, or off / medium / maximum on the V1 models that have levels), HydroJet jets, panel lock and errors, depending on the model; pool filter pumps: power, filter timer, cartridge to change, errors.
- Commands shown at once and kept until the cloud reports them (the clouds take 5 to 30 seconds), checked by a reading 15 seconds later; heating also starts the filter pump, as the spa does.
- Readings every 30 seconds to 5 minutes; the session of each account kept in /data, a refused login waits 15 minutes, then longer, so a wrong password never gets the account locked.
- Heating speed learned from the heating curve, and the time the water will be ready.
- Dashboard widgets "Spa" (water, target, heater, ready time, filter, bubbles, jets, errors; buttons to heat, for the bubbles and to change the target by one degree) and "Spa curve" (water and target over 1, 3 or 7 days, heating speed).
- Scene triggers: the water reaches its target, the spa reports an error, the spa is unreachable, the spa answers again.
- Scene actions: read the spa (temperatures, heater, ready time, errors) and heat the spa to a temperature (target and heater in one step).
- Configuration button "Test the accounts".
- Texts in English, French, German and Spanish.
